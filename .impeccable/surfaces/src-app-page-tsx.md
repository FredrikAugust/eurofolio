# Eurofolio overview surface

- Scope: authenticated `/` overview on desktop and mobile.
- Mode: Operate.
- Audience and job: one owner checks total wealth, custodian distribution, asset allocation, largest positions, valuation trust, and full holdings quickly.
- Proof: real Turso portfolio values, observation dates, provider names, completeness state, and native-currency details.
- Constraints: read-only web UI; MCP-only mutations; EUR master currency; server-rendered except the lazy chart; no fabricated history.
- Direction: Quotation Specimen. Variable-foundry proof meets exchange quotation board.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/challenger-specimen.png`.
- Memorable moment: the EUR total behaves like the specimen’s dominant glyph line while every smaller fact locks to its exposed measurement grid.
